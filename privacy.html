Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026. Plain language first, legal precision where it counts.

What we collect

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which pages they read, and which product links they click. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city or region, derived from IP address), device and browser type, and referring site. We also record when someone clicks an affiliate link, logging only the retailer and product page — not who you are. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. We use it only to improve the site; we don't use it for ads or build advertising profiles.

Email addresses (coming soon). Our notify list isn't live yet, so the sign-up box doesn't store anything today. When it launches, we'll store your email address only to send updates about new episodes, comparisons and picks; every email will include an unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe by writing to hello@mitothrum.com. We'll update this policy to name the email provider we use.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we keep the correspondence to answer you.

What we don't collect

No ad pixels, no data sales. We run no advertising pixels or retargeting trackers. We don't sell personal information, we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't share it with data brokers.

The concierge chat stays in your browser. Our on-page concierge answers from a fixed set of editorial answers using JavaScript that runs entirely on your device. Your questions are never sent to our servers.

Your choices: turning off analytics

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out and don't load Google Analytics at all. You can also opt out on this browser with the button below, or use Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Affiliate links

Product links on this site may be affiliate links, including Amazon Associates links. When you click through and buy, the retailer may set its own cookies and process the purchase under its own privacy policy. We receive only aggregate commission reports — never your name, address, or payment details.

Health information

Mitothrum publishes educational wellness content, not medical advice. Please don't enter personal health details into the concierge chat or our email forms — we're a shopping guide, not a medical advisor.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at hello@mitothrum.com. We'll respond within 30 days and won't treat you differently for asking. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU), you may have additional rights under local law; write to us and we'll honor them.

How long we keep data

Google Analytics keeps user-level data for the retention period set in our Analytics account; aggregate reports may be kept longer. Email correspondence is kept as long as needed to answer you.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and note the change here for 30 days.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@mitothrum.com.
