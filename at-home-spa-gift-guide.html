Before you gift: three quick checks

Heat and devices aren’t universal. Infrared blankets, sauna tents, microcurrent and LED masks all list people who should skip them or ask a doctor first, including during pregnancy and for anyone with an implanted device. Each pick below says who; if you’re not sure, choose from the bath, scent or sleep sections instead.

Skin is personal. Strong, acidic vitamin C and retinol can sting sensitive skin. When you don’t know their routine, the gentler option is the safer gift.

Scent is shared. A diffuser fills the whole room, so think about who else lives there. Some essential oils are a concern around pets; check with a vet if they have one.

Questions, answered

What’s a good at-home spa gift when I don’t know their routine?

Pick something that adds to a ritual without changing their skincare: a bath soak with a shower oil, or an essential oil set with a diffuser. Active skincare and devices are personal; if you go that way, choose the gentler option or include a gift receipt.

Is a sauna blanket a good gift?

For someone who already wants one, yes. As a surprise, check first: infrared heat isn’t for people who are pregnant, have cardiovascular conditions or heat sensitivity, or have implanted devices, and it needs a place to lie down and storage space.

Is an LED mask or microcurrent device a safe surprise?

Not always. Microcurrent devices list several reasons not to use them, including pregnancy, a pacemaker or other implanted device, epilepsy and recent facial surgery. LED masks call for a doctor’s advice for people on light-sensitizing medication or with eye conditions. If in doubt, gift the choice.

Why don’t you show prices?

Prices change constantly, especially around Black Friday, and a stale price misleads. Tiers ($ smaller gift, $$ mid-range, $$$ splurge) are relative to everything on this page; the current price is one click away.

Can they return it?

Return terms depend on the seller and the listing, and holiday return windows vary. Check the listing before you buy, and keep the gift receipt.
