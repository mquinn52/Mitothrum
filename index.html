How we work

What the Science Actually Says

Episode 1: Infrared saunas

Episode 1 · ~6 minutes

The honest summary: the strongest sauna science we have — twenty years of Finnish data on heart and brain health — comes from traditional hot-air saunas, and it is observational: compelling and dose-responsive, but not proof.

Infrared itself has genuine clinical trial data in heart failure through Waon therapy — but that was a supervised medical setting, not a living room. For at-home infrared wellness claims, the evidence is still thin, and no long-term mortality data for infrared exists at all.

We include the 2023 randomized trial that found no vascular improvement after eight weeks of sauna — because the null results are part of the story. And we cut every detox claim: there is no peer-reviewed basis for them.

Sources: Laukkanen et al., JAMA Internal Medicine, 2015 (Finnish cohort); Tei et al., Circulation Journal, 2016 (WAON-CHF trial); Debray et al., Journal of Applied Physiology, 2023 (8-week sauna RCT).
